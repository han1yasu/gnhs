Key Features

Everything the Guidance Office Needs

A complete solution managing student concerns from submission to resolution.

AI-Assisted Summarization

Automatically summarizes lengthy concern reports so counselors quickly understand key issues.

Digital Concern Reporting

Students and teachers submit concerns through a secure online form — anytime, from any device.

Anonymous Reporting

Students may submit sensitive concerns anonymously for issues like bullying or harassment.

Case Severity Classification

Cases are classified as Low, Medium, or High Priority so counselors respond appropriately.

Analytics Dashboard

Visualize patterns in student concerns and generate reports for school administration.

Notification System

Real-time alerts for session schedules, case status changes, and guidance office responses.
